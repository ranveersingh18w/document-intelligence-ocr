## Conclusion :

<|ref|>text<|/ref|><|det|>[[72, 510, 950, 710]]<|/det|>
The teaching games conducted at Saraswati Shiksha Niketan U.P.S. Sallada from 11/07/2025 to 26/07/2025 were a successful and valuable experience. The children participated actively and enjoyed the games. Simple and interesting games such as Musical Chair, Aunty Race, In-Out, Statue Game, Lemon and Spoon Race and Passing the Ball helped in the overall development of the children. These activities improved their confidence, concentration, teamwork, discipline and physical coordination. The programme showed that children can learn many useful qualities through simple and enjoyable games.
